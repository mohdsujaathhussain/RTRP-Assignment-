Malla Reddy Engineering college And Management Sciences

About:

Malla Reddy Engineering College is a premier institution located in Hyderabad, India. Established in 2002, it offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and management. The college is known for its state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and strong emphasis on research and innovation. With a commitment to academic excellence and holistic development, MREC aims to prepare students for successful careers in various fields. The college also fosters industry collaborations and provides ample opportunities for internships and placements.

Vision: To be a globally recognized institution that nurtures innovative thinkers and leaders in engineering and management.
